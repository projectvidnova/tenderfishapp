Product

Four cores. One platform.

Everything a project controller needs — from first tender to final invoice — in one unified workspace.

Core A — Tender Builder

AI-assisted creation of tender packages. Upload plans and specs, and Tenderfish extracts line items, assigns DIN cost groups, and drafts your LV in minutes.

Core B — Supplier & Comparison

Invite suppliers, collect bids in a branded portal, and auto-generate a Preisspiegel with AI-flagged outliers and negotiation recommendations.

Core C — Budget & Margin Engine

4-layer budget tracking (Baseline → Forecast → Committed → Variations) with real-time margin indicators and automated approval workflows.

Core D — Workspace Inbox

Unified inbox that captures emails, documents, and supplier responses — AI classifies, routes, and links every message to the right project context.

Why Tenderfish

Built for German construction.

Every feature is designed around HOAI fee structures, DIN 276 cost groups, and VOB regulations.

AI That Speaks HOAI

Claude-powered pipelines trained on German construction standards. Extracts items from PDFs, assigns DIN codes, and suggests pricing.

GAEB Import & Export

Native support for GAEB X83/X84 exchange formats. Import existing LVs and export for any AVA system.

Multi-Gate Approvals

Configurable approval workflows with thresholds, delegation rules, and complete audit trails.

Site Reporting

Capture progress photos, notes, and evidence directly from the construction site. Automatic GPS tagging and timestamping.

Enterprise Security

Row-level security, SSO via SAML/OIDC, complete audit logging, and EU-hosted infrastructure. GDPR compliant.

Real-Time Analytics

Live dashboards for budget status, tender progress, and supplier performance across all your active projects.
